INTERNAL RUNBOOK: Patch Management and Vulnerability Remediation

Source-ID: patch-management
Classification: internal_runbooks

## Overview
This runbook defines VRA's internal patch management process for all production systems.

## Patch Priority Tiers
- CRITICAL (CVSS >= 9.0, KEV-listed): Patch within 24 hours. Notify CISO immediately.
- HIGH (CVSS 7.0-8.9): Patch within 7 days. Schedule maintenance window.
- MEDIUM (CVSS 4.0-6.9): Patch within 30 days. Bundle with quarterly update cycle.
- LOW (CVSS < 4.0): Patch within 90 days or at next major release.

## Pre-Patch Checklist
1. Confirm patch authenticity (verify GPG signature or vendor checksum).
2. Test patch in staging environment for minimum 2 hours before production.
3. Take snapshot/backup of affected system.
4. Notify change management team via ticket.
5. Schedule maintenance window (minimum 30-minute window for critical patches).

## Patch Application Steps
1. Apply patch during scheduled window.
2. Verify service restarts cleanly.
3. Run automated smoke tests.
4. Confirm vulnerability is no longer present (re-scan with Nessus/OpenVAS).
5. Update the VRA job status to PATCHED.
6. Document in change management system.

## Rollback Procedure
If patch causes service degradation:
1. Restore from snapshot immediately.
2. Open P1 incident ticket.
3. Notify vendor for emergency support.
4. Apply compensating controls (network isolation, WAF rule).
